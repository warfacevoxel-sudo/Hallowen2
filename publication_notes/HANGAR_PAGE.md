# 🎃 Halloween Plugin

**Turn your Paper server into a haunted house for the season.** Random scares, summonable pumpkin bosses, an
animated grave ritual with three undead waves, Herobrine encounters, pumpkin buffs, a candy economy, statistics
and a Discord webhook – one lightweight plugin for **Paper, Spigot and Purpur 1.21.3 – 26.x**. Free and open
source (MIT).

## What it does

- **Scare events** – pumpkin curse, Slenderman, harmless fire and lightning, darkness, a four-stage **Herobrine** at night. Each with its own interval and switch.
- **Boss battles** – fence/lapis altar + carved pumpkin, lit with flint & steel: **Pumpkin Zombie Boss** and **Halloween Skeleton Boss** with minions, health bars, titles and configurable loot.
- **Grave system** – build a grave, light the candle, watch the 18-second ritual, then fight **three waves of buffed undead**. Wave bar, team XP, glowing reward chest.
- **Candy economy & Pumpkin Trader** – Halloween Candy drops from Halloween mobs, bosses and graves; any villager becomes a trader with the trades you define.
- **Pumpkin buffs, bat witches, pumpkin-headed mobs.**
- **Statistics & leaderboards** – `/halloween stats`, `/halloween top`, PlaceholderAPI expansion, Discord webhook.
- **Runs itself** – season scheduler (on/off by date), witching hour, WorldGuard flag `halloween-events`, world blacklist, per-player opt-out.
- **Rewards your way** – items, potions, candy, console commands and Vault money, each with a chance.
- **Every text editable** – 170+ categories in `messages.yml`, `&`/hex colours, updates never overwrite your changes.

## Install

Drop the jar into `plugins/`, restart, edit `plugins/Halloween_Softici/config.yml` and `messages.yml`,
`/halloween reload`. Optional hooks: Vault, PlaceholderAPI, WorldGuard.

## Compatibility

Paper, Spigot, Purpur · **Minecraft 1.21.3 – 1.21.11, 26.1, 26.2** (one jar) · 26.3 expected to work (tested on
Paper 26.3 alpha) · Java 21+ · not Folia. Upgrades from 3.0.5+ are drop-in (config smart-merge).
