[SIZE=6] 
[COLOR=#ff6600][B]Halloween Plugin[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]

Bring the spooky spirit of Halloween to your Minecraft server!
This plugin adds fun, scary features designed to [B]surprise your players[/B] and create a creepy seasonal atmosphere.

Developed by [B]Softici s.r.o.[/B]

[B][COLOR=#ff9933]──────────────────────────[/COLOR][/B]

[SIZE=5][COLOR=#ff9933]Features[/COLOR][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*][B][COLOR=#999999]Spooky Login Greeting[/COLOR][/B] 
Players are welcomed with a chilling message when they join: 
[ICODE]Spooky times have arrived... Type /halloween disable if you don't dare to be cursed.[/ICODE]

[*][B][COLOR=#ff6600]Pumpkin Curse[/COLOR][/B] 
- Every 1–10 minutes (configurable), a carved pumpkin appears on players' heads. 
- Items are safe — the plugin checks inventory space before swapping helmets. 
- After 3 curses, players' free slots fill with stacks of pumpkins (Easter egg). 

[*][B][COLOR=#999999]Slenderman Prank[/COLOR][/B] 
- An Enderman spawns 1 block in front of a random player and vanishes after 0.5s. 
- Creepy sounds + server-wide messages. 

[*][B][COLOR=#ff3300]Fire Prank[/COLOR][/B] 
- Harmless fire effect for a few seconds (no damage). 
- Broadcasts funny lines like: 
[ICODE]I fell into the burning ring of fire...[/ICODE]

[*][B][COLOR=#ffff00]Lightning Prank[/COLOR][/B] 
- Harmless lightning strike near a random player. 
- Thunder and cave ambience included. 

[*][B][COLOR=#000000]Darkness Event[/COLOR][/B] [COLOR=#ff0000]NEW in v1.4.0![/COLOR]
- Brief moment of blindness (1 second) with creepy atmosphere.
- Multiple layered trapdoor sounds + cave ambience.
- Random spooky messages broadcast to all players.
- Default 10-50 minutes between events.

[*][B][COLOR=#33cc33]Configurable Pumpkin Buffs[/COLOR][/B] [COLOR=#ff0000]NEW in v1.3.0![/COLOR]
- Wearing a carved pumpkin grants customizable beacon-like buffs
- Default: Haste II, Strength II, Speed II, Resistance II, Jump Boost II, Regeneration II
- [B]Fully customizable:[/B] Add/remove effects, change levels, toggle individual buffs
- 8 admin commands for real-time buff management ([ICODE]/halloween buffs[/ICODE])
- Supports ALL Minecraft potion effects (Night Vision, Fire Resistance, etc.)
- Toggle glowing effect independently
- Player glows with an eerie aura (configurable)

[*][B][COLOR=#ff9900]Progressive Cake Rewards[/COLOR][/B] [COLOR=#ff0000]NEW in v1.4.0![/COLOR]
- Wearing pumpkin helmet rewards cakes progressively
- 1st reward: 3 minutes → Cake
- 2nd reward: 6 minutes → Cake
- 3rd reward: 9 minutes → Cake
- Continues increasing by 3 minutes up to 30 minutes
- After reaching max: Cake every 30 minutes
- Players notified: "Next reward in X minutes!"

[*][B][COLOR=#00ccff]Server-Wide Announcements[/COLOR][/B] 
- All pranks broadcast randomized spooky messages to everyone. 
- Messages are fully customizable in [ICODE]messages.yml[/ICODE].
- [B]Auto-merging:[/B] Updates automatically add new messages without overwriting yours!
[/LIST]

[B][COLOR=#ff9933]──────────────────────────[/COLOR][/B]

[SIZE=5][COLOR=#ff9933]Configuration[/COLOR][/SIZE]

Prank intervals are stored in [ICODE]config.yml[/ICODE]:

[CODE=yaml]
enabled: true

intervals:
  pumpkin:
    min: 1
    max: 10
  slenderman:
    min: 10
    max: 15
  fire:
    min: 30
    max: 60
  lightning:
    min: 20
    max: 40
  darkness:
    min: 10
    max: 50

# Configurable pumpkin buffs (v1.3.0+)
pumpkin_buffs:
  enabled: true
  glowing: true
  effects:
    - type: HASTE
      level: 1
      enabled: true
    - type: SPEED
      level: 1
      enabled: true
    # ... customize as needed
[/CODE]

Prank messages are customizable in [ICODE]messages.yml[/ICODE]. Example:

[CODE=yaml]
slenderman:
  - "Slenderman almost got %PLAYER%... but vanished in shame."
fire:
  - "I fell into the burning ring of fire..."
darkness:
  - "%PLAYER% felt the darkness creeping in..."
  - "Something lurks in the shadows around %PLAYER%..."
[/CODE]

[B]Auto-Merging:[/B] When you update the plugin, new messages and config sections are automatically added without overwriting your customizations!

[B][COLOR=#ff9933]──────────────────────────[/COLOR][/B]

[SIZE=5][COLOR=#ff9933]Installation[/COLOR][/SIZE]

[LIST=1]
[*]Place the plugin jar in your [ICODE]plugins/[/ICODE] folder.
[*]Start the server once to generate configs.
[*]Edit [ICODE]config.yml[/ICODE] and [ICODE]messages.yml[/ICODE] if desired.
[*]Use [ICODE]/halloween reload[/ICODE] or restart the server.
[/LIST]

[B]Upgrading from older versions:[/B]
Simply replace the JAR and restart. Your configs and messages will be preserved, and new features will be automatically added!
