[SIZE=6][B][COLOR=#FF6600]❓ FAQ[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]

[SPOILER="Frequently Asked Questions"]
[B]Q: Can players disable Halloween for themselves?[/B]
A: Yes! Players can use [B]/halloween disable[/B] to opt-out completely. They won't receive events, messages, pumpkins, or be able to spawn bosses.

[B]Q: Do bosses work on modded servers?[/B]
A: Yes, as long as the server runs Spigot/Paper API 1.20+.

[B]Q: Can I customize the boss stats?[/B]
A: Absolutely! Health, damage, speed, and XP rewards are all configurable in [B]config.yml[/B].

[B]Q: What happens to bosses when Halloween is disabled globally?[/B]
A: All active bosses despawn, all minions are removed, minion tasks stop, and all pumpkin traders revert to normal villagers instantly.

[B]Q: Can high-level players exploit XP?[/B]
A: No! The smart XP system scales rewards based on player level. Players below level 30 get direct levels (3-7 levels), while players above level 30 get raw XP that scales naturally with Minecraft's exponential curve.

[B]Q: Do I need any dependencies?[/B]
A: Nope! This is a completely standalone plugin.

[B]Q: Can I change the messages?[/B]
A: Yes! All messages in [B]messages.yml[/B] are fully customizable with support for unlimited random variations.

[B]Q: How do I prevent pumpkin farming on join?[/B]
A: The plugin includes a 30-minute join cooldown automatically. Players who recently joined can't get another pumpkin helmet until the cooldown expires.

[B]Q: Can I disable specific features?[/B]
A: Yes! Every major feature (bosses, trading, events, buffs, random mobs) has an enabled toggle in config.yml.

[B]Q: Is this compatible with other plugins?[/B]
A: Yes! No known conflicts with major plugins. Works alongside economy plugins, protection plugins, and more.

[B]Q: How do minions work with the Halloween Boss?[/B]
A: The Halloween Boss spawns a Cursed Bat every 10 seconds and a Haunting Phantom every 60 seconds. Only one of each type exists at a time (old ones despawn when new ones spawn). All minions despawn when the boss dies.

[B]Q: Can disabled players still spawn bosses?[/B]
A: No! Players who have disabled Halloween cannot spawn bosses and will see a helpful message telling them to enable Halloween first with [B]/halloween enable[/B].

[B]Q: What happens to Pumpkin Traders when I update the plugin?[/B]
A: Existing Pumpkin Traders persist through updates. When you disable Halloween globally with [B]/halloween off[/B], they automatically revert to normal villagers.

[B]Q: Why is the Halloween Boss a Skeleton instead of a Witch?[/B]
A: In v1.5.3, we changed from Witch to Skeleton based on player feedback. The Skeleton is more balanced, killable with standard gear, and provides better combat engagement.

[B]Q: Do prank events affect disabled players?[/B]
A: No! Players who have disabled Halloween ([B]/halloween disable[/B]) don't receive any events, messages, or effects.

[B]Q: Can I turn off broadcasts so only affected players see messages?[/B]
A: Yes! Set [B]broadcast_events: false[/B] in config.yml to make messages private (only the affected player sees them).

[SPOILER="Common Issues & Solutions"]
[B]"Missing message: darkness" error?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Delete [B]messages.yml[/B] and restart, or update to v1.4.0+ which auto-merges messages
[/LIST]

[B]Buffs not applying?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Check [B]/halloween buffs list[/B] - is [B]pumpkin_buffs.enabled[/B] true?
[*]Ensure player is wearing a CARVED_PUMPKIN (not jack-o-lantern)
[*]Try [B]/halloween reload[/B]
[/LIST]

[B]Darkness effect not visible?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]v1.4.0+ uses BLINDNESS effect which is more visible. Update to latest version.
[/LIST]

[B]Commands not working?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Verify you have [B]halloween.use[/B] permission for admin commands
[*]Check console for error messages
[/LIST]

[B]Bosses not spawning?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Ensure Halloween is enabled globally ([B]/halloween on[/B])
[*]Check that player hasn't disabled Halloween ([B]/halloween enable[/B])
[*]Verify [B]pumpkin_monster.enabled[/B] or [B]pumpkin_witch.enabled[/B] is true in config
[*]Make sure you're using the correct summoning structure (fence+pumpkin or lapis+pumpkin)
[/LIST]

[B]Minions not spawning?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Update to v1.5.3+ for fixed minion spawning
[*]Check [B]bat_spawn_interval[/B] and [B]phantom_spawn_interval[/B] in config
[*]Minions only spawn during combat (boss must be attacking/taking damage)
[/LIST]

[B]XP rewards too low/high?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Adjust [B]xp_reward[/B] values in config (10 for Pumpkin Monster, 15 for Halloween Boss)
[*]The smart XP system automatically scales with player level
[*]For low-level players (< 30): rewards give direct levels
[*]For high-level players (≥ 30): rewards give raw XP that scales naturally
[/LIST]

[B]Pumpkin Trader not converting?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Ensure [B]pumpkin_villager.enabled[/B] is true
[*]Use CARVED_PUMPKIN (not jack-o-lantern)
[*]Right-click the villager with the pumpkin
[*]Check console for error messages
[/LIST]

[B]Join cooldown not working?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Update to v1.5.4+ for join cooldown feature
[*]Cooldown is 30 minutes and tracked in [B]data.yml[/B]
[*]Players see remaining time on join
[/LIST]
